Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 8, 2026

Bullet Brigade (the “Game”) is published by Ashutosh Papnoi, operating as Iron Sights Studio.. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game.

Please replace every item in square brackets before publishing this policy. If your data practices, advertising partners, online features, or audience change, update this policy and your Google Play Data safety answers before releasing the update.

1. Information We Handle

The Game does not require an account or ask you for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment-card information.

The Game stores the player name you choose, game settings, selected character, unlocked content, and control preferences locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this local profile data.

When you use local multiplayer, the Game uses local-network communication between participating devices. Other players in that local session may see the player name you choose and your in-game activity. Do not use personal information as your player name.

2. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to show banner and rewarded advertisements. AdMob and its advertising technology may collect or receive information such as device identifiers (including Advertising ID), IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interaction and diagnostic information, and other information described in Google’s privacy documentation. This information may be used to provide, measure, secure, limit the frequency of, and personalize ads where permitted.

For users in regions where consent is required, the Game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to present Google’s applicable advertising consent choices before live ads are initialized. We do not use the first-launch acknowledgement in the Game as a substitute for advertising consent.

3. In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional one-time in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment information; we do not receive or store your payment-card or bank details. Google Play may provide the Game with purchase status, product identifiers, order-related identifiers, and purchase tokens so that purchased content can be delivered and restored. Purchase records may also be retained by Google under its own policies.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Game does not use browser cookies. Advertising and billing providers may use device identifiers, SDK storage, pixels, cookies, or similar technologies for the purposes described in their own policies.

5. Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services, which process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads — advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and consent management.
  • Google Play Billing / Google Play — purchase processing, purchase restoration, and fraud prevention.

We do not currently use an analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, account system, or cloud-save service. If we add one, we will update this policy and our Google Play disclosures before it is released.

6. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the Game, save your local preferences and entitlements, provide and measure advertisements, process and restore purchases, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information for money. Information collected by Google services may be shared with Google and its service providers, advertising partners, or authorities as described in Google’s policies or when legally required. Local multiplayer information is shared directly with the other devices in your local game session.

8. Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it by clearing the Game’s app data or uninstalling the Game. We do not maintain a developer-operated database of player profiles. Google retains advertising, consent, and purchase data under its own retention practices and legal obligations.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing depends on consent. Because we do not maintain player accounts or a developer-operated user-data database, you can delete the Game’s local data through your device settings. For information handled by Google, use Google’s privacy controls or contact Google.

For requests or questions relating to information we directly control, contact us using the details below. We may need information to verify and respond to a request as permitted by law. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

10. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising configuration and Play Console target-audience settings must match the Game’s actual intended audience. If you believe a child has provided us personal information directly, contact us and we will address the request as required by law.

11. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the Game’s design, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Use official Google Play releases and keep your device updated.

12. International Processing

Google and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Google describes its international data-transfer safeguards in its privacy policy.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the Game, our data practices, or legal requirements. We will post the updated version at this URL and change the effective date. Material changes will be communicated where required by law.
